# ambient
Slow, textural, beatless music built on drones and soft pads. Used for relaxation, sleep and deep focus. Low energy, high acousticness.
# blues
Twelve-bar structures, expressive guitar bends and weary, honest vocals. Themes of heartache and resilience. Slow to mid tempo.
# classical
Orchestral or solo acoustic instrumentation with dynamic range. Suits studying, reflection and formal or sacred moods. Usually instrumental.
# country
Storytelling lyrics, twangy guitars and warm vocals about home, road and love. Often nostalgic and rustic.
# edm
Four-on-the-floor beats, big synth drops and festival energy. High tempo and danceability, euphoric and pumped feel.
# folk
Acoustic guitar and intimate vocals, narrative songwriting. Tender, rustic and nostalgic, low to mid energy.
# hip-hop
Rhythm-led with rapped vocals, heavy bass and confident delivery. Good for workouts and bold, motivational moods.
# indie pop
Bright but understated pop with dreamy production and personal lyrics. Mid energy, feel-good and slightly wistful.
# jazz
Improvisation, swung rhythms and rich chords. Smooth and warm at night or in a cafe; romantic and relaxed.
# latin
Rhythmic percussion and dance grooves, often sung in Spanish. Party, summery and euphoric, high danceability.
# lofi
Dusty, mellow beats with warm samples, made to sit in the background. Cozy study and rainy-day music, usually instrumental.
# metal
Distorted guitars, fast drums and powerful vocals. Aggressive, heavy and intense; very high energy.
# pop
Polished hooks, catchy choruses and broad appeal. Upbeat, bright and feel-good with high danceability.
# r&b
Smooth grooves and soulful vocals about love and desire. Sensual, late-night, mid energy.
# reggae
Offbeat guitar skank, laid-back bass and sunny vibes. Relaxed, warm, summery.
# rock
Guitar-driven, live-band energy with a driving rhythm. Rebellious and intense at high energy, anthemic at mid energy.
# synthwave
Retro 1980s synths, neon-lit nocturnal driving mood. Nostalgic, steady and cinematic.
